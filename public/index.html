About

A strategist who writes code.

I am Ljupcho Shemov, an aviation expert from North Macedonia. Since 2008 I have worked at the Ministry of Transport and Communications in Skopje, today as Deputy Head, shaping the strategic plan and economic development of civil aviation and turning traffic forecasts, regulation and market data into policy that moves a country's air connectivity forward.

Alongside aviation, I build software. As a freelance full-stack .NET developer and SRE engineer, I design web applications end to end and keep them fast, observable and always on. The same discipline that keeps aircraft safe keeps production systems healthy.
